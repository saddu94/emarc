About EMARC INTERNATIONAL

Building global connections, constructing modern landscapes, and delivering procurement excellence with a vision to grow bigger and better.

Who We Are

EMARC INTERNATIONAL PRIVATE LIMITED is a diversified business group headquartered in Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala, India. We support businesses through international trade, procurement, construction, real estate development, and related operational services.

Our team of experienced professionals brings more than 10 years of industry expertise across strategic resourcing, supply chain management, logistics, construction, real estate, and business operations. Together, these capabilities form an integrated business network that provides coordinated support for a wide range of projects and business needs.

Guided by our philosophy, “Think Bigger, Grow Better,” we focus on reliable, efficient, and tailored solutions that help businesses grow. Our registered CIN is U52292KL2025PTC092152, reflecting our commitment to corporate excellence, transparency, and long-term value creation.

Our corporate goals are structured to drive progress, facilitate seamless trade, and build robust infrastructures for a sustainable future.

02
